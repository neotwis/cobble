# Cobble – Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** October 31, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Cobble is a browser extension designed to provide a simpler and more personal browsing experience.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Cobble accesses, how it is used, and how it is handled.

## 2. Information We Access

Depending on the features you use, Cobble may access:

- **Browser Bookmarks:** To display and manage your bookmarks within the extension.
- **User Preferences:** Settings and customization preferences saved by the extension.
- **City Names:** Locations manually entered by users to retrieve weather information.

Cobble does not intentionally collect personal information such as names, email addresses, passwords, or payment details.

## 3. How We Use Information

Information accessed by Cobble is used only to provide its features:

- Display bookmarks when the user enables the bookmark feature.
- Save extension settings and preferences.
- Retrieve weather information based on the city entered by the user.
- Improve the functionality and reliability of the extension.

## 4. Local Storage

Extension settings and preferences may be stored locally using the browser's storage functionality.

Bookmark information is accessed through the browser's bookmarks permission and is not intentionally uploaded to Cobble's servers.

## 5. Third-Party Services

Cobble may communicate with third-party APIs, such as weather or other public data services, to provide certain features.

When a user requests information, relevant query data may be sent to the corresponding service.

These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. Cobble does not control how they process information.

## 6. Data Sharing

Cobble does not sell users' personal information.

Information is not intentionally shared with third parties except when necessary to provide features requested by the user, such as retrieving weather information through an external API.

## 7. Permissions

Cobble may request the following browser permissions:

- **Storage:** Save extension settings and preferences.
- **Alarms:** Schedule background tasks when required.
- **Notifications:** Display relevant extension notifications.
- **Active Tab:** Temporarily access the active tab when a feature requires it.
- **Bookmarks (Optional):** Access bookmarks when the user enables bookmark-related features.

Permissions are used only for their intended extension functionality.

## 8. Data Security

Cobble is designed to minimize the information it accesses and uses. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Cobble is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Cobble evolves. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
